EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

One app instead of five. Built around how your school already works.

A calendar that keeps itself current

Connects to the school calendar your office already keeps. Families only see the events for their own children.

Alerts that reach every family

Post once from any computer and push it to the whole school, one division, or one class, with read receipts.

Faith at the center

A Chapel Corner with weekly memory verses, chapel reminders, prayer requests, and church and school events together.

Class feeds and messaging

Teachers share photos and practice sheets, message parents directly, and lead classroom parent groups, with quiet hours built in.

A Student Corner for every age

Avatars and stickers for little ones, agendas for elementary, and planners, teams, and class discussions for older students.

Works with what you already use

Grades, tuition, and lunch stay in your student information system, such as FACTS. Students sign in with their school Google accounts.

One system. Every device your school uses.

Everything stays in sync, whether a teacher starts on a computer and finishes on an iPad, or a family checks in from the car line.
