Website privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy covers the gatheredforschools.com website. Gathered for Schools™ is a product of Pathways to Excellence Coaching & Consulting L.L.C., doing business as SmoothRunStudio, a Georgia company.

What we collect

When you request a founding spot, we collect the information you type into the form: your name, role, school name, email address, and school size. We do not use advertising trackers or sell any information.

How we use it

We use it only to contact you about Gathered for Schools, such as scheduling a walkthrough or sharing founding-school details.

Who handles it

Form messages are delivered to us through Formspree, a form service. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts for its lettering. These services may record basic technical information, such as your IP address, to operate and protect their services. See our full list of service providers.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing privacy@gatheredforschools.com.

The Gathered for Schools app

The app itself, including student and family information, will have its own privacy policy, shared with each school before launch.
